WHOOP Health Intelligence Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

WHOOP Health Intelligence is a personal health analytics application created for private, non-commercial use. The application connects to WHOOP with the user's explicit authorization.

Information accessed

The application may access WHOOP profile information, body measurements, recovery data, physiological metrics made available through recovery records, cycles, sleep, and workouts, limited to the permissions authorized by the user.

How information is used

The information is used only to provide personal health, fitness, recovery, sleep, training, and longitudinal trend analysis for the authorized user.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are not published in this website or source repository. Application secrets are stored separately as protected environment variables. OAuth tokens are stored by the application for the purpose of maintaining authorized access.

Sharing and sale of data

Personal WHOOP data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising purposes.

Third-party services

The application relies on WHOOP to provide authorized health and fitness data and may use secure infrastructure and artificial-intelligence services to process data for the user's personal health intelligence experience.

Revoking access

The user may revoke the application's access through WHOOP or discontinue use of the application. Stored application data may also be deleted by the application owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application evolves or additional integrations are added.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to the application owner.
